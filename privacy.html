Privacy policy

Tradéx · last updated October 11, 2026

Tradéx helps you keep track of your trading card collection. This page explains what we store, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, and a password if you create one. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed) by our sign-in provider, and we can never see them.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address from your Google account. We don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Your collection: the cards, binders, notes, prices and photos you add, so they can sync across your devices.

If you use Tradéx as a guest, your collection stays only in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your collection in sync on your devices.
  • To send account emails, such as verifying your email or resetting your password.

We don't sell your information, show you ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it's stored

Your account and collection are stored with Supabase, and the site runs on Railway. Each person's collection is locked so that only their own account can read or change it. Card photos you upload can be opened by anyone who has the photo's link.

Your choices

  • You can ask us for a copy of everything we store about you.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it, and we will.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
